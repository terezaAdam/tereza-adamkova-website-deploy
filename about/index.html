Bio

Tereza Adámková is a Czech photographer, creative director and quiet web maker working between Prague and Berlin. Her work sits at the edge of editorial and cinema — atmospheric, restrained, made by hand.

She works in small, careful teams for fashion houses, hotels, perfumers and independent publishers, and keeps a parallel practice of personal films and books.

Education: Academy of Fine Arts, Prague (MFA, Photography, 2019). Features in: The Gentlewoman, Apartamento, IGNANT, It's Nice That.

Years

  • 2018First commissioned editorial. Begins working in 35mm.
  • 2020Moves to Prague. Founds a one-person studio above the river.
  • 2022Direction for first hotel campaign. First short film released.
