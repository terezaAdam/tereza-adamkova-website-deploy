Bio

Hi, I’m Tereza, a photographer, videographer and digital strategist. My work does not fit neatly into one category. I create photographs and videos focused on authentic moments.

Alongside my creative work, I work in digital marketing. My role includes social media, photography, video production, content strategy, website development, and the coordination of digital projects. I also design and build websites, helping individuals and organisations turn their ideas into clear online experiences. Contact me for collaborations or to discuss your project.
